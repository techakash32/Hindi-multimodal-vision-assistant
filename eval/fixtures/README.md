# Evaluation fixtures

All 70 images are **synthetic**, generated by `python -m eval.generate_dataset` from a fixed seed with PIL and OpenCV
(DejaVu fonts). They are not photographs. The ground truth is exact because it is the text that was drawn.

| Prefix | Count | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `medicine_*` | 12 | label text on paper: clean, low contrast, noisy, rotated 10 and 20 degrees, partly under a black bar, small print |
| `sign_*` | 12 | white-on-colour signs: clean, blurred, night-time (dark and noisy), tilted |
| `menu_*` | 8 | serif menu lines, two columns, faded print, rotated; "handwriting" is per-letter jitter on an italic font (no handwriting font is available offline) |
| `scene_*` | 20 | dark room with flat coloured blocks as "person", "chair", "dog", "bus". The demo detector (`visionassist.demo.BlobModel`) finds persons and chairs by colour; `dog` and `bus` blocks have no class in it, so those scenarios are expected to fail with `detection_miss` |
| `ambiguous_*` | 10 | ordinary frames paired with a question that has no usable target ("What's that?", "Is it safe?") |
| `negative_*` | 8 | deliberately bad inputs: heavy blur, text the same grey as the board, a black bar over the only text line, a nearly black frame, a blurred scene, a request to read *and* describe, upside-down text |

`negative_abstain_006` and `negative_abstain_007` have **no image on purpose**: they model "the camera delivered no
frame", so `input.image` is `null`. No ground truth is attached to any abstain scenario.

Not generated, because it cannot be done honestly offline: real photographs, real handwriting, Devanagari text, motion
blur, audio clips. `voice_command_*` scenarios use typed or hand-written transcripts (`simulated_asr: true` marks the two
mis-transcriptions); no audio files exist. To measure ASR latency put five `.wav` clips in `eval/audio/`.

What the images can and cannot show: they test the pipeline logic, the guardrails and OCR on clean rendered text. They say
little about real-world accuracy, and the image-quality thresholds in `quality.py` were chosen from these same fixtures.
